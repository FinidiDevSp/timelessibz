Privacy Policy — TIMELESS IBZ

Last updated: October 10, 2026

"TIMELESS IBZ Publisher" is an internal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel TIMELESS IBZ to manage their own channel. It is not offered to the public and is not used by third parties.

1. What data it uses

With the channel owner's explicit authorization, the tool uses the YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API) only to:

  • Upload the channel's own videos with their title, description, chapters, tags, thumbnail and captions.
  • Manage the privacy, scheduling, playlists and the channel's own comments on those videos.
  • Read aggregated statistics about the channel itself (views, watch time, retention, likes, subscribers) to decide when to publish and which sessions work best.

It does not collect personal data from viewers or any third party.

2. Where it is stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the channel owner's computer. They are never sold, shared, sent to third-party servers or used for advertising.

3. Google services

The tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page. When it is revoked, the locally stored data is deleted.
